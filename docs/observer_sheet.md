# Observer tally sheet

Print one sheet per day. Fill one row per break window. Condition is the main condition (passive, inviting, complimenting); the photo action does not matter here. Stand a few metres from the booth and
do not interact with participants.

**Definitions**
- **Passed by**: walked within about 3 m of the robot.
- **Approached**: stopped or looked at the robot for 2 seconds or more.
- **Took photo**: pressed the photo button and had a photo taken (count each person in a group photo).

Use tally marks during the window. Total them afterwards and type them into `analysis/observations.csv`
with the columns `date,start,end,condition,passed_by,approached,took_photo,notes`.
